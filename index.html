How to Play

W A S D: move · Mouse: look · Space: jump
Double-tap Space: creative flight
Shift: sneak / descend · R: sprint

Left click: mine / attack · Right click: place / use
Mouse wheel or 1–9: hotbar · Middle click: pick block
E: inventory · Escape: pause · F3: debug

Inventory: search by name, choose a category, then click a block to put it in the selected hotbar slot. Hover or tap to see names.

On touch screens, drag the left joystick to move, drag the world to look, and use the action buttons.

Levers and redstone blocks power adjacent pistons, droppers, and dispensers. Most additional catalog entries are decorative cube blocks; stairs, fences, doors and other named variants do not yet have their full shapes or special behavior.

World edits autosave in this browser when storage is available. Export a backup to keep your builds.
